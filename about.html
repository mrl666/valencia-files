What is Valencia Files?

Valencia Files is a free, English-language briefing for people who live in or around Valencia. Every edition gathers the news, services, and practical information that affect daily life here, and presents it in a single page you can read in about five minutes.

Who is it for?

Anyone in the Valencian Community whose first language is not Spanish — expats, students, remote workers, families, and visitors staying long enough to need real information rather than tourist highlights.

What's inside

  • Lead story — one notable item from the Generalitat Valenciana or the Ajuntament de València
  • Valencia, Alicante, Region — recent news from the city councils and neighbouring towns
  • Events — upcoming activities from the Diputació de València agenda
  • Sport — recent results for Valencia CF, Villarreal CF, Levante UD, and CD Castellón
  • Weather — live conditions for Valencia, Alicante, and Castellón
  • Admin & deadlines — upcoming tax deadlines from the AEAT
  • Resident desk — quick links for emergency, transport, health
  • Expat essentials — a tabbed guide to NIE, empadronamiento, healthcare, and more

How it's made

Valencia Files is produced by an automated pipeline that runs three times a day. It collects content from official sources — city councils, the Generalitat Valenciana, the AEAT, AEMET, and public-sector feeds — translates the headlines into English, and publishes the edition as a single page plus a PDF archive copy.

The pipeline runs without human intervention. When a source changes or becomes unreachable, the affected section falls back to the last known good version rather than breaking the edition.

Sources and attribution

All content is drawn from official public-sector sources and reused under Spain's Law 37/2007 on the reuse of public sector information. Every item on the page cites its original source. Photographs used on the site are individually licensed for reuse and credited where required.

What it is not

Valencia Files is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any council, the Generalitat Valenciana, or any other public body. The information published is informational only and is not a substitute for official notices, legal advice, or emergency services.

Corrections

If you spot an error — a mistranslation, a wrong date, a broken link — please let us know. Corrections are made as soon as possible.
