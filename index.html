Expat essentials official resources
  • Empadronamiento (town hall registration) — Certificado de empadronamiento (online, by phone 010, or in person). For cita previa for padrón trámites.
  • Healthcare (SIP card) — Request cita previa for atención primaria online, by phone, or at your centro de salud.
  • Tarjeta Sanitaria Europea (TSE) — Apply or renew online at the Seguridad Social. Free, arrives in 5 days.
  • Driving licence exchange — Canje de permisos extranjeros at DGT. Check if your country has an agreement.
  • Fallas — Official municipal information and news on Fallas from the Ajuntament de València.
  • Valencian language — Official resources from the Acadèmia Valenciana de la Llengua.
  • Local holidays — Calendario laboral from the Generalitat.
  • School enrolment (escolarización) — Oficina Municipal de Escolarización (OME) for information on centres, deadlines, and documentation. Phone: 96 352 54 78.
  • Escolarización sobrevenida — For students arriving mid-year. Online application via PortES.
  • Escoletes infantiles (0–3 years) — Municipal nursery schools. Information on Cabanyal-Canyamelar escoleta and others.
  • Housing advice (Infovivenda Solidària) — Free information on rental rights, obligations, and subsidies. Oficina d'Infovivenda Solidària, Carrer Amadeu de Savoia, 11.
  • Red Alquila (rental agency) — Agencia de Alquiler offering housing search, advice, and rental guarantees.
  • Tax deadlines — Official AEAT taxpayer calendar.
  • Social Security number (NUSS) — Apply online via Importass or the Seguridad Social app.
  • Travelling with pets into Spain — Requirements: EU Regulation 2016/429 (BOE). Microchip, rabies vaccine, EU pet passport.
  • Correos — customs on parcels — Official customs information for parcels from outside the EU. From July 2026: fixed €3 duty for items under €150.
  • Modelo 720 (overseas assets) — Official AEAT procedure for declaring assets held abroad.
  • Beckham Law (special tax regime) — Official Modelo 149 to opt into the regime for displaced workers.
  • DANA and flood preparedness — Official 112 Comunitat Valenciana emergency information.
  • Beach and sea conditions — AEMET weather warnings for the Valencian coast.
  • Summer closures — August is the main holiday month. Most shops, clinics, and offices close for at least part of August.
