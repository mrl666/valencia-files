Get in touch

Valencia Files is automated, but a human reads every message. Whether you have a correction, a tip for a story we should cover, a suggestion for a new section, or just a question — write to us.

Email
hello@valenciafiles.es

What to write about

  • Corrections — a mistranslation, a wrong date, a broken link. Tell us the edition and what's wrong.
  • Story tips — something happening in Valencia that English-speaking residents should know about.
  • Source suggestions — an official council, agency, or public body we should be tracking.
  • Feedback — what works, what doesn't, what's missing.

What we can't do

  • Provide legal, tax, immigration, or medical advice. Consult a qualified professional.
  • Answer questions about specific council procedures — your local ayuntamiento or the Generalitat is the right place.
  • Reply instantly. The pipeline runs three times a day, and messages are answered when time allows.

Privacy note

If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply. We don't add you to any list, and we don't share your address with anyone. Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation.
